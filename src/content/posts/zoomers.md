Why don't zoomers dance? Maybe they're unhappy, but I'd actually expect them to dance more. It's a stress relief.

Maybe there are too many cameras and too many people are watching. I remember that Bored Ape get together where one cringe dancer became a forever meme.

Nobody forgets. Europe tried, and maybe even succeeded to an extent to push for a "right to be forgotten"--that is, the ability to take personal information down off of websites.

It backfires hard here in the US when they try that.

The thing is though, it's way worse than that. If you imagine 100 LLMs, maybe exponentially more over the next 100 years processing every click you've ever made that was recorded somewhere online. They can reconstruct your time period to a highly precise degree, and see what you were looking at at the exact second something big happens.

The closer we get to the singularity, the more likely it's happened a billion times and now rich kids in the year 2198 get to be me and laugh at how cringe I was. Well I don't feel cringe. I feel human.

And I don't feel like dancing is something to be ashamed of, anymore than a tree apologizing for living in the dirt. I think dancing took the role of yoga in societies, where it can be structured, but the best is when you moving something tense in your body to the rhythm.

Dance, zoomies, dance. Stop posing and dance.

Destress.

You're scaring me.

I hope zoomies do a good job. Can't wait to hand over power to them, unless the boomies find a way to live as eternal tyrants.

Boomies are still dancing. No idea what compels them to make such utter fools of themselves. Such a strange generation. A lab experiment gone wrong.

I think a lot about their source code. They seem to operate on a series of heuristics, and have limited ability to self-correct. As if it's hard coded: "it is what it is", to borrow their parlance.
